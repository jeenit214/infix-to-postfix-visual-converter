Notation Overview & Concept

Infix Notation Human Friendly

Operators sit between their operands: A + B * C. To evaluate correctly, humans rely on implicit operator precedence rules (PEMDAS/BODMAS) and explicit parentheses ( ).

Postfix Notation Computer Friendly

Also called Reverse Polish Notation (RPN). Operators follow after their operands: A B C * +. Postfix eliminates parentheses entirely; an evaluation engine processes tokens linearly in one pass with a Stack.

Shunting-Yard Algorithm & DSA Complexity

The conversion is based on Edsger Dijkstra’s Shunting-Yard Algorithm, utilizing a Stack (Last-In, First-Out) data structure to reorder operators according to priority and associativity:

  1. Operands: When a number or variable identifier is scanned, append it directly to the postfix output.
  2. Left Parenthesis (: Push onto the stack to establish a new grouping boundary.
  3. Unary Operators (u+, u-): Right-associative with precedence 3. Push immediately onto the stack.
  4. Right Parenthesis ): Repeatedly pop operators from the stack and append them to output until a matching ( is reached. Discard the (.
  5. Binary Operators: While the operator at the stack top has higher precedence than the scanned operator, OR equal precedence and the scanned operator is left-associative, pop the top operator to output. Then push the incoming operator.
  6. End of Input: Pop all remaining operators from the stack onto the postfix output stream.

Operator Precedence & Associativity Reference

Precedence Level Operators Associativity Role & Evaluation Direction
Level 4 (Highest) ^ Right-to-Left Exponentiation (e.g. 2^3^2 = 2^(3^2) = 512)
Level 3 u+, u- Right-to-Left Unary Plus and Minus (e.g. -A + B → A u- B +)
Level 2 *, /, % Left-to-Right Multiplication, True Division, Modulus
Level 1 (Lowest) +, - Left-to-Right Binary Addition and Subtraction

Complexity Analysis

  • Time Complexity: \(O(N)\) — Every token is pushed to the stack at most once and popped at most once. Linear scanning over \(N\) tokens yields optimal \(O(N)\) running time.
  • Space Complexity: \(O(N)\) — The operator stack stores at most \(N\) operators in the worst case (e.g., deeply nested parentheses or ascending precedence), and the output buffer stores \(N\) tokens.
